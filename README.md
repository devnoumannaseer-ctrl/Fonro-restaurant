# FORNO — Restaurant & Artisan Kitchen

> *"Crafted by Fire. Perfected by Tradition."*

A luxury-minimal, editorial restaurant website for **FORNO**, featuring authentic Pakistani Desi Murgh Chanay, Siri Paye, fresh Tandoori Roghni Naans, and indulgent Cheezious fusion delights, paired with an interactive 3D spice & tarka parallax background.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Brand Identity & Pato Place Style Hero**:
   - Distinctive pill-bordered brand wordmark: **`FORNO RESTAURANT`**.
   - Hero header with calligraphic script: *"Welcome to"* + bold uppercase headline **`FORNO PLACE`**.
   - Rounded pill CTAs: **`LOOK MENU`** and **`Order on WhatsApp`**.
   - Hero carousel slider with `<` and `>` arrow controls and pagination dots.
2. **Interactive 3D Desi Culinary Atmosphere (No Pizza on Front Page)**:
   - A traditional **Royal Brass / Copper Handi (Deg)** with sizzling golden Desi Ghee tarka sheen, fresh ginger juliennes, and coriander garnish.
   - Tumbling 3D aromatic spices: **Star Anise (Badian Khatai)**, **Whole Dried Red Chilies**, **Green Cardamom Pods**, and **Fresh Coriander Leaves**.
   - Live rising golden tarka embers drifting upward with mouse parallax depth (Kumo style).
3. **Desi Food Menu with Category Chips & High-Res Photos**:
   - **Interactive Category Chips**:
     - *All Items*
     - *Desi Murgh Chanay*
     - *Paye & Maghaz*
     - *Cheezious & Cheezy Fusion*
     - *Tandoor & Naans*
     - *Sides & Add-ons*
     - *Lassi & Drinks*
   - Authentic Pakistani dishes with Urdu script titles, portion sizes, spice badges, and high-resolution photography.
4. **Direct WhatsApp Ordering & Table Reservation**:
   - Working order cart drawer with itemized WhatsApp message generator.
   - Interactive table reservation modal accessible from the header.
   - Free of third-party delivery links.
5. **Modern Tech Stack**:
   - React with Vite, Three.js, React Three Fiber, and Framer Motion.
   - Code-split vendor bundles and lazy-loaded 3D scene.
